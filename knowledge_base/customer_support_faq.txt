CUSTOMER SUPPORT KNOWLEDGE BASE

1. ORDER STATUS
Customers can ask for their order status using their order ID.
Order information may include order status, tracking number,
and estimated delivery date.

2. REFUND POLICY
Customers who receive damaged or defective products can contact
customer support to request a return or refund.
The support team should verify the order and applicable policy
before confirming refund eligibility.

3. RETURN POLICY
Customers can contact customer support to initiate a return.
The support team should verify the order, return eligibility,
and applicable return conditions before confirming a return.

4. TECHNICAL SUPPORT
Customers experiencing product problems should describe the issue
and provide the product name or model.
The support agent should suggest relevant troubleshooting steps.
If the issue continues, the customer should contact technical support.

5. PRODUCT RECOMMENDATIONS
The product agent should ask about the customer's requirements,
budget, and preferred features before recommending a product.

6. CUSTOMER COMMUNICATION
The chatbot should respond politely and clearly.
It must not invent order details, refund approvals, or company policies.
If the knowledge base does not contain the required information,
the chatbot should explain that the information is unavailable
and recommend contacting customer support.
